ABHISHEK PRAJAPATI · FOUNDER & CEO

THE EXPERIENCE BEHIND THE APPROACH

Seeing the whole business.
Staying close to the work.

Abhishek’s experience brings together operational transformation, performance management and commercial execution.

His work centres on translating strategic priorities into a practical operating structure: clear objectives, connected KPIs, defined responsibilities and a review rhythm that helps teams act on what the data reveals.

Across process improvement and business operations, he has focused on how work moves between people and functions. That means examining handoffs, diagnosing inefficiencies, aligning accountability and developing systems that support consistent execution as an organization grows.

Exposure to commercial functions adds an essential perspective: stronger processes must serve the business. Execution discipline and growth priorities need to reinforce one another, with performance improvements connected to tangible business outcomes.

His approach is analytical, hypothesis-driven and grounded in implementation. The aim is to embed change within everyday processes, management systems and organizational culture so that new ways of working can endure.
