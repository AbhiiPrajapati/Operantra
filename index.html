A FEW PRACTICAL ANSWERS

Clear from
the beginning.

Can we begin with just one function?

Yes. Define a single function, territory or workflow first. The engagement can expand after scope, performance and operational fit are reviewed together.

How is this different from supplying manpower?

The engagement covers management of an agreed function: team supervision, workflows, reporting and follow-through. Recruitment and deployment support that responsibility.

Do we need to change our systems or facilities?

The operating model can work within client-owned facilities and existing systems. Tool access, workflows and any required changes are agreed during scoping.

How are fees and service levels decided?

A proposal is scoped around the function, locations, workload, team requirements and agreed reporting. Commercial terms and service levels are agreed before work begins.

Who approves purchases, credit and regulated work?

Approval authority stays with the client unless expressly delegated. Collections cover follow-up and coordination. Any regulated certification, legal work or specialist approval requires appropriately authorized professionals and a defined scope.
